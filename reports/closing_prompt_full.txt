21.1 Proof gaps (is the edge real?)

2.  Candidate-level report. Mean net R for all candidates, taken only and rejected only, by year and setup, with week-block intervals. State what ranks candidates, and add a test that permuting future data leaves the ranking unchanged.

Charge costs on breakeven exits and fill gaps below the stop at the open.
